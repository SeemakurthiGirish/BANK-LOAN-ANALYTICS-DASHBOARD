# Bank Loan Analytics Dashboard 🏦

An interactive **Bank Loan Analytics Dashboard** built in **Power BI**, designed to help loan officers and business leaders track loan performance, risk segmentation, and application trends across the organization.

---

## 🔍 Overview

This dashboard analyzes **38,576 loan applications**, providing a 360° view of funded amounts, repayment behavior, and borrower risk profiles. It enables stakeholders to identify good vs. bad loans, monitor interest rate and DTI trends, and understand application patterns by purpose, term, and demographics — supporting data-driven lending decisions.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Power BI Desktop** – Data modeling, DAX measures, and visualization
- **DAX** – Custom calculated measures (DTI Ratio, Interest Rate, Funded vs Received, etc.)
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation

## 📊 Dashboard Features

**Summary Page**
- **Good Loan vs Bad Loan Split** – Donut charts showing 86.18% good loans vs. 13.82% bad loans
- **Good Loan Breakdown** – 33,243 applications, 370.2M funded, 435.8M received
- **Bad Loan Breakdown** – 5,333 applications, 65.5M funded, 37.3M received
- **Funded Amount vs Repayment** – Comparison across Fully Paid, Charged Off, and Current loans
- **Loan Application by Status** – Application counts, average interest rate, and average DTI ratio broken down by Fully Paid, Charged Off, and Current
- **Interactive Filters** – Filter by loan purpose (Car, Credit Card, Debt Consolidation, Educational, Home Improvement, House, Major Purchase, Medical, Moving, Renewable Energy, Small Business, Vacation, Wedding)

**Overview Page**
- **Loan Application by Month** – Trend line showing steady growth from 2.3K (January) to 4.3K (December)
- **Loan Application by State** – Geographic map view of applications across North America
- **Loan Application by Term** – Donut chart comparing 36-month (26.2K) vs. 60-month (10.3K) terms
- **Loan Application by Home Ownership** – Bar chart: Rent (18,439), Mortgage (17,198), Own (2,838), Other (98), None (3)
- **Loan Application by Purpose** – Treemap led by Debt Consolidation (18K), followed by Credit Card, Other, Home Improvement, Major Purchase, and Small Business

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **86.18%** of loans are classified as "Good Loans," reflecting a healthy overall repayment performance
- **Debt Consolidation** is by far the leading loan purpose, accounting for roughly half of all applications
- Borrowers on **36-month terms** significantly outnumber those on 60-month terms (26.2K vs. 10.3K)
- **Renters and mortgage holders** make up the vast majority of applicants, with outright homeowners a much smaller share
- Loan applications show **consistent month-over-month growth** across the year, nearly doubling from January to December
